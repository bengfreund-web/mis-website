SHAPE Support

MIS is committed to physical education in Montana. Our partnership with Society of Health And Physical Educators (SHAPE) goes beyond being the largest financial supporter of the organization, but the desire to ensure that our educators have the best support, experience and professional development opportunities afforded to them.
