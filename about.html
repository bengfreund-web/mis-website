Montana Institute of Sport Leadership

AB
Ali Behnam
Board President
Founder and managing partner of Riviera Partners, an executive search firm specializing in early-stage, venture-backed technology companies. Before Riviera Partners, he co-founded Whitetruffle. Earned his B.A. in Economics and Statistics from the University of California, Irvine. Featured in Business Insider’s “10 People Who Can Get You The Best Jobs In Tech And Make You Rich.”
NS
Nick Simpson
Board Member
A 26-year career in Private Banking, beginning in 1998 at Citi Group Private Bank in London. Joined First Republic Bank in 2005 as a Private Bank Manager, leading a team of 150 bankers and growing the book of business from $100 million to over $15 billion across 12,000 households. Currently National Director of Citizens Private Bank.
RB
Robert (Bobby) Beers
Board Member
A Missoula, MT native and 1996 graduate of Western Montana College. Three-time all-conference wide receiver and All-American. Coached at San Jose State and Montana State, then the Berlin Thunder of NFL Europe (two World Bowl titles). Athletic director at Rocky Mountain College 2009-2015. Now owns and manages several businesses.
GA
Guy Almquist
Board Member
A prominent figure in the financial landscape of Helena, Montana. 15+ years at First Interstate Bank in Helena, now Market President there. Graduated Carroll College in 1997; played basketball and earned a spot in the Saints Athletic Hall of Fame. Still coaches Helena Capital High School’s boys’ varsity basketball team.
EB
Emilie Bydwell
Board Member
Head Coach of the U.S. Women’s National Rugby Team, which recently secured the United States’ first Olympic medal in women’s rugby. Former member of the U.S. Women’s National Sevens Team, playing in three Rugby World Cups. Focused on building a sustainable athlete pathway and advancing the women’s game at every level.
