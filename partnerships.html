The 26 Club: An Exclusive Community

The 26 Club (T26C) is an exclusive, invite-only community focused on increasing physical activity. Its name is inspired by the statistic that only 26% of young people get the recommended daily amount of physical activity. Membership is limited to 26 like-minded individuals, invite-only, with a $5,000 annual membership fee supporting the “TRY Sport” initiative. As of late 2024, The 26 Club had 16 members.

Members are invited to high-level professional sporting events — recent trips included a USA vs. Canada rugby game in Los Angeles (with the USA Rugby National Team Head Coach) and a trip to Edinburgh with Scottish international players. The next scheduled event is Ireland vs. New Zealand in Chicago, first weekend of November.
